# Phase N-3.2: Vessel Positive Case Selection

## Candidate Search Strategy
I conducted a targeted search of NOAA IncidentNews, NTSB marine investigation reports, U.S. Coast Guard records, and SkyTruth/Cerulean public reports for the Gulf of Mexico across the requested 2022–2023 temporal window. 

The search targeted:
- Vessel/barge/tanker origins
- Open Gulf / offshore environments
- Major volume (>1,000 gallons) to ensure SAR visibility

## Discovered Incident Summary (2022–2023)

1. **Main Pass Pipeline Spill (Nov 2023)**
   - *Source:* Subsea pipeline (Third Coast Infrastructure).
   - *Feasibility:* Rejected. Infrastructure source, not a vessel.

2. **Flint Hills Terminal Break (Dec 2022)**
   - *Source:* Pipeline/Terminal.
   - *Feasibility:* Rejected. Coastal terminal/pipeline, not an offshore vessel.

3. **Terrebonne Bay Collapse (Aug 2022)**
   - *Source:* Structural collapse of a Hilcorp Energy oil tank platform.
   - *Feasibility:* Rejected. Infrastructure source.

4. **Sabine-Neches River Tow Sinking (Mar 2023)**
   - *Source:* Towing vessel sinking.
   - *Feasibility:* Rejected. Inland river environment, not open Gulf. 

5. **Ashland Mississippi River Spill (Jun 2023)**
   - *Source:* Vessel discharge of asphalt-like oil.
   - *Feasibility:* Rejected. Inland river environment, lacks open-water drift physics applicability.

6. **Cedar Bayou Abandoned Tugs (Jun 2022)**
   - *Source:* Abandoned tugs.
   - *Feasibility:* Rejected. Inland bayou.

*Note:* Major open-water ship collisions resulting in significant spills occurred in 2014 (Texas City "Y") and May 2024 (Galveston Pelican Island Bridge), both falling strictly outside the requested 2022–2023 window.

## Conclusion & Feasibility Assessment
A comprehensive review of official spill response records confirms that during 2022 and 2023, the Gulf of Mexico did not experience a major (>1,000 gallon), open-water oil spill definitively originating from a vessel or barge. All major documented offshore releases during this period originated from fixed infrastructure (pipelines and platforms). While illegal "bilge dumps" routinely occur, they are typically low-volume, unconfirmed by physical Coast Guard sampling, and lack publicly documented exact coordinates/timestamps required for guaranteed Sentinel-1 alignment.

As such, it is impossible to satisfy the strict combination of:
1. Documented vessel origin
2. Offshore Gulf of Mexico
3. 2022–2023 timeframe
4. Sufficient volume for guaranteed SAR visibility

## Final Status
NO SUITABLE VESSEL POSITIVE CASE FOUND
